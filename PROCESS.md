  > **Expense Overview** — *One view for everything you spend, in every currency, from every account.*
  >
  > **What it is.** Expense Overview takes your OTP and Revolut bank exports - a personal EUR account, a personal HUF account, and a joint account you share with someone else - and merges them into one categorized dashboard. Every currency converts to HUF automatically. Every joint expense counts only your fair half toward your own totals, with a separate view showing the full picture of the shared account. If you ever pay for something that someone partially reimburses you for, you can mark exactly that - your income and spending numbers stay honest instead of getting inflated by money that was never really yours.
  >
  > **Who it's for.** You share a household with someone else, and you're tired of mentally recalculating who owes what. You use more than one bank. You regularly use an account in a currency you don't normally think in.
  >
  > **How it's different.** vs. your banking apps - OTP and Revolut each only show their own account; this merges all of them, in every currency, and automatically halves a joint expense before it hits your personal totals. vs. a spreadsheet - most people building this kind of overview copy transactions in by hand; this reads your bank's own export files directly, categorizes automatically, and remembers every correction you make even after you upload a newer export. vs. Splitwise-style apps - those ask you to manually log who owes what; this starts from your real joint-account transactions and lets you correct the ones that aren't genuinely shared, like a reimbursement that would otherwise look like random income.
