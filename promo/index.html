Why this, and not something else

vs. your banking apps
OTP and Revolut each only show their own account. This merges all of them, in every currency, and automatically halves a joint expense before it hits your personal totals.
vs. a spreadsheet
Most people copy transactions in by hand. This reads your bank's own export files directly, categorizes automatically, and remembers every correction you make, even after you upload a newer export.
vs. Splitwise-style apps
Those ask you to manually log who owes what. This starts from your real joint-account transactions and lets you correct the ones that aren't genuinely shared, like a reimbursement that would otherwise look like random income.
